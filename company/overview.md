# Fernway in one page

## What we sell

Fernway Shifts: shift planning and labour-cost forecasting for independent café and restaurant groups with 3 to 20 locations. Managers build schedules, the tool checks them against local break and overtime rules, and projects the wage bill before the week is published.

## To whom

| | Today |
|---|---|
| Accounts | about 40 |
| Paying venues | about 140 |
| Countries | Germany, the Netherlands, Ireland |

One account can have many venues: see `GLOSSARY.md`.

## Why customers pick us

Competitors are built for enterprise chains and take weeks to configure. Our pitch: a venue is live in under a day. Onboarding speed is the whole positioning, which is why T2L is the number the team watches (typical today: 1 to 3 days, see `customers/onboarding.md`).

## Price

€39 per venue per month, 15% off for annual prepay. Anything lower is a Mara-check. Details: `customers/pricing-and-discounts.md`.
