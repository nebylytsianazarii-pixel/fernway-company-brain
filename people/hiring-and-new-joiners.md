## A new joiner's first week (until there is a real process)

Today nobody owns this: whoever has time that week walks the new person through things. Until that changes, the brain is the walkthrough:

1. Read `README.md`, then `company/overview.md` and `company/team-and-owners.md`.
2. Read `GLOSSARY.md`. Account vs venue is the mistake every new hire makes.
3. Read the `customers/` doc for your team's step of the customer path.
4. Ask Claude your questions in this repo before you interrupt a person.
5. Anything you could not find or that turned out wrong: open an issue or a pull request. Fresh eyes are how the brain finds its gaps.
